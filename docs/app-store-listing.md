## Description

**EN**

Lily is a friendly AI nutrition and fitness companion that keeps your day in order without the usual logging chores.

Just tell her what you ate. Type it, say it, or send a photo. Lily estimates calories and protein, learns your regular foods, and shows how much you have left for the day.

• Log meals, workouts, steps and weigh-ins by chat — text, voice notes or photos
• See your daily balance: calories and protein left, net of workouts
• Lily remembers your usual foods and preferences
• Gentle reminders and an evening summary, at the times you choose
• Fix or delete anything with a message ("make it 55 minutes", "remove the beer")
• Your data stays yours: delete your account and everything in it at any time from Profile
• Optional: link WhatsApp to chat with Lily there too. The app works fully without it.

Lily is an AI. She can make mistakes, and she is not a dietitian, nutritionist or doctor. Nothing here is medical advice.

## הערות לבודק של אפל (App Review Notes)

> Lily is a nutrition and fitness companion. Users log meals, workouts, steps and weigh-ins by chatting with an AI assistant inside the app (text, voice notes, photos).
>
> **Sign in:** please use **Sign in with Apple**. No demo account is needed.
>
> **WhatsApp** (Profile → WhatsApp bot) is an optional extra channel for the same chat. All features are available in the app without it, so it does not need to be tested.
>
> **Microphone and camera** are used only when the user taps the mic button or attaches a meal photo.
>
> **Account deletion:** Profile → Delete my account.
>
> The assistant is an AI and is not a medical device or medical advice. The app is free; there are no in-app purchases in this version.
